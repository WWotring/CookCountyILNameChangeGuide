Adult Name Change Guide For Cook County IL

Written 05/14/2026


Why I am publishing this guide / Notes
- Many folks don't have the bandwidth to navigate self-file on their own
- Volunteer organizations with real lawyers (https://www.tjlp.org/) exist to fill that gap but the demand is high and (I am told) it is slow
- I hope my research and multiple failures can help others who decide to just do this already
- When my initial filing failed, the number I was given to call for help was great - short wait, helpful clerks, no phone trees or robots. I called twice while getting this figured out and both times it was so so easy.
- I haven't completed the process yet, so updates are possible. But, I have successfully filed the case and all my documents so this should be complete.


What this guide does not address:
- For folks who intend to file a waiver of the substantial fees, I did not do this so I will not cover it. 

Getting started - Generating the forms
- This was the easiest part for me because Illinois Legal Aid Easy Form walked me through it.
- https://www.illinoislegalaid.org/legal-information/name-change-adult

Gathering Documentation
- You will need these 2 pdfs, but not right away.
	- your birth certificate
	- images of both sides of your IL drivers license or ID. This must have a Cook County address. You will also need to redact all but the last 4 digits of your ID number. 

Read the documentation to be sure there are no other details you may need to address
https://www.illinoiscourts.gov/documents-and-forms/approved-forms/circuit-court-standardized-forms-suites/name-change/
- Click on Name Change Adult to display the contents of that section
	- The "How to change your name" documentation here was very helpful and you should read it first to be sure this process applies to you, and to be prepared for next steps.
	- The other items are the forms you have already completed.

Efile
https://ilcourts.info/efile
- you will make an account if you, like me, have never needed to file before
- Start Filing
- Start New Case
- Case Information
	- Court location: Cook County - County Division - District 1 - Chicago
	- Case Category: Miscellaneous
	- Case type: Name Change
	- Save this and move on
- Parties
	- Petitioner
		- I am this party - click this to indicate you are
		- Your current legal name
		- Your address
		- Attorney Information: Pro Se (this means you are doing it yourself without an attorney)
	- Name Sought
		- The name you want to have
		- your address again
		- Pro Se
- Filings
	- This was the tricky part because it was hard to get real info about the details.
	- Click to file your first document. 
		- Select efile only - this type of case does not need other parties served
		- Filing code - search name change - Complaint / Petition - Petition for Name Change
		- Upload your request pdf.
		- Security: non-confidential
			- If you file a motion to impound to keep your name change a secret the court clerk will change this to confidential for this and your later documents, you always choose non-confidential.
		- Save this filing
	- Click + Add More
		- Selest efile only
		- Filing Code: Exhibits - Filed
		- Upload the order for name change pdf
		- Save
- Services
	- ignore this. skip to fees.
- Fees
	- You will enter a payment method
	- You will select your legal name as the Filing Party
- Review and Submit
	- Exactly what it sounds like.
	- Change anything last minute and click submit

What's next?
- If all went well your filing will be accepted and a case will be opened
- You will now have a telecourt date! Congrats!
- This is when the rest of your documents will be requested - your birth certificate, your IL ID or Drivers license, and if wanted your Motion to Impound and Order to impound.
	- Obviously, if the clerk tells you to file your documents in a way other than what I outline, follow their instructions. These are what I was told but I would not be surprised if there is variance.

- Login.
- On the home screen, click Filing History
- On the Envelope with your accepted filings, click the dropdown in the upper right corner. Choose File Into Case
- You will be back in the filing screen we know from before
	- Click to add a file
		- Choose efile only
		- Filing Code: Exhibits - Filed
		- Upload your birth certificate pdf
		- Save
	- Click + Add More
		- Choose efile only
		- Filing Code: Exhibits - Filed
		- Upload your IL ID or drivers license pdf - remember to include both sides, id number redacted
		- Save
	- Click + Add More - If you are also filing a motion to impound
		- Choose efile only
		- Filing Code: Motion to - Filed
		- Upload your Motion to impound and order to impound, saved as a single pdf
		- Save
